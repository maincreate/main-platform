Devices missing from a complete scan become offline. Records disappear from the default list after 7 days and move to the archive after 30 days; returning devices are recognized and restored. No inventory record is automatically deleted.

Device compatibility

A matching USB identifier suggests a device family; it does not verify its exact chip, board, flash layout or supported firmware image. Flashing is disabled until read-only chip verification and artifact compatibility are implemented.

Manual flashing and automation

Devices on this page are discoverable, not automatically flashable. A safe flash operation must bind a verified complete firmware image, exact chip family, authenticated builder, current serial port, and a user-approved job. The device must be revalidated immediately before writing.

Not enabled yet: flashing, unattended flash rules and commit-triggered device writes. ESP-IDF build jobs and artifacts are available under Jobs.
