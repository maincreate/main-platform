Manual flashing and automation

Devices on this page are discoverable, not automatically flashable. A safe flash operation must bind a verified complete firmware image, exact chip family, authenticated builder, current serial port, and a user-approved job. The device must be revalidated immediately before writing.

Not enabled yet: flashing, unattended flash rules and commit-triggered device writes. ESP-IDF build jobs and artifacts are available separately in Jobs & Builders.
